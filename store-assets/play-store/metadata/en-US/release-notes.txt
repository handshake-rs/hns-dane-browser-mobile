0.5.10 adds native wallet lifecycle controls, protected confirmed-wallet deletion, and read-only balance, distinct payment and name-transfer receive targets, history, names, and status fields, which remain unavailable without a scoped indexed backend. It also adds ECH for supported HNS origins, clearer sync/reset recovery, and code-split asset-burst handling. Android corrects WebView headers and safe-area padding and prevents service-worker cache misses from using a blocking whole-body fallback.
