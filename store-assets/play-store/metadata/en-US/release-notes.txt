1.0.0 introduces the Shakescape name and visual identity while preserving the existing app identity and data. It adds native wallet lifecycle controls, protected local deletion, read-only balance, history and name views, plus exact-text name import; read and import remain unavailable without a scoped indexed backend. It also adds ECH for supported HNS origins and improves sync recovery, WebView headers, safe-area layout, and service-worker streaming.
