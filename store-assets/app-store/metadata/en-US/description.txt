Shakescape brings ICANN and Handshake websites into one browser and shows how each connection was verified.

Browse:
- Open ordinary HTTPS sites while Handshake headers synchronize
- Enter Handshake names such as example/ and name.tld/
- Verify Handshake headers and name proofs on your device
- Inspect resolver paths, DNSSEC, TLSA, DANE, and WebPKI security details
- Use optional HNS peer-to-peer DNS relay or DNS-over-HTTPS recovery
- Manage tabs, history, downloads, cookies, and website data in WebKit

Wallet and names:
- Create or restore a device-local HNS and Bitcoin wallet
- Sync HNS balances and activity directly with Handshake peers
- Sync Bitcoin with compact filters and relevant blocks from Bitcoin peers
- Receive HNS by address or payment QR code, and review guarded HNS and Bitcoin sends
- Track Handshake names, edit resource records, and transfer or finalize names
- Delete the local wallet when needed

ShakeScape offers and swaps:
- Connect to peers and publish, inspect, or cancel signed BTC/HNS offers
- Follow noncustodial BTC/HNS atomic swaps through funding, redemption, or refund
- Resume swap progress after an interruption

Wallet keys stay on your device. Websites cannot read the wallet or start native wallet actions. Sends and swap funding require your approval in the app. QR scanning is processed on the device. No sign-in is required.
