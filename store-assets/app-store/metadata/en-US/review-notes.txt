Shakescape needs no account or sign-in. It combines a WKWebView browser with a native, noncustodial HNS and Bitcoin wallet. Websites cannot access the wallet. The app supports iPhone, iPad, and compatible Apple-silicon Macs.

Browser review:
1. Launch the app. The https://shakescape.com/ homepage opens while Handshake headers synchronize.
2. Enter another ordinary HTTPS address. The page and address bar update, and the security label shows WebKit WebPKI verification during header synchronization.
3. Enter https://shakescape/ to inspect a Handshake page. The bundled header snapshot supports proof-backed resolution while foreground synchronization continues; the security label shows the HNS resolver and DANE validation path.
4. Open Settings to inspect resolver traces, proof details, and browser controls. HTTP pages are marked insecure.

Wallet review:
1. Open Settings, then Handshake wallet. A fresh install shows wallet creation and restoration choices. If testing creation, record the recovery phrase to complete its confirmation step.
2. After unlock and peer synchronization, the wallet shows HNS and Bitcoin balances, receive addresses, a payment QR code, guarded HNS and Bitcoin sends, and tracked-name actions. Value-changing actions require native review and approval.
3. The ShakeScape card can connect to relay.learnhns.com:12038 for signed BTC/HNS offers and atomic swaps. Swap transactions need funded test wallets and show their funding deadline, stage, redemption, and refund status.
4. The camera permission appears when the native QR scanner is opened. QR decoding stays on the device.

WKWebView uses an authenticated 127.0.0.1 loopback proxy; the app installs no VPN and changes no system proxy. While Handshake headers synchronize, sites under permanently reserved ICANN roots can use WebKit WebPKI; Handshake names wait for verified headers. This build accepts incoming HTTP/HTTPS URLs and does not include the managed default-browser entitlement.
