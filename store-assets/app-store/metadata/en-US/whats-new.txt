Fixes fresh wallet creation and restoration on iOS by enforcing the native wallet's protected, owner-only storage boundary. New wallets use the hsd/Bob-compatible BIP-44 payment branch, while an explicit legacy restore option preserves access to earlier Shakescape phrases. Recovery verification now hides the phrase and uses stronger random choices. This update also includes coordinated direct peer discovery, board replication, swap recovery, matched swap notifications, automatic FINALIZE tracking, accessible controls, iPad and compatible Mac support, and direct browser-link handling.
