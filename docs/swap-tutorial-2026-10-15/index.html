How long this swap took

1 hour 25 minutes 16 seconds elapsed from the first wallet check at 8:51:26 PM to both phones showing 5 of 5 at 10:16:42 PM. Both participants were online and responding promptly. Block confirmations and wallet scans account for much of the elapsed time.

Timeline labels show elapsed time between captures when the next action switches phones.

Remote peers: funding and refund deadlines
  • This offer was listed for 48 hours. A new acceptance requires a full 24-hour funding window before the listing expires, so this listing could accept a peer for roughly its first 24 hours.
  • After acceptance, the signed session allows 24 hours for funding. The first Bitcoin lock must begin with at least three hours left in that window. The phones require one Bitcoin confirmation before HNS funding, then two HNS confirmations before its claim.
  • If a funded swap cannot complete, the HNS lock becomes refundable around 48 hours after session creation and the Bitcoin lock around 72 hours after it. Handshake timelock rounding can shift the exact times; each execution shows its signed deadlines.

An absent peer or delayed chain confirmation can extend the exchange to the signed refund deadlines.

What moved between the wallets

Before and after balances for this swap, including funding and claim fees on each chain. The 4,330-sat Bitcoin lock paired a 4,000-sat signed fee cap with the 330-sat minimum receiver output. Pixel paid 1,560 sats to fund it. Another swap may require a higher cap and lock at the live fee rate.
