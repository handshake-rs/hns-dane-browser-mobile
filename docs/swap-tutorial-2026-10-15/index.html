Moto sells HNS · Pixel pays Bitcoin

Swap HNS for Bitcoin on two phones

Follow the swap in event order. Moto's actions appear on the left and Pixel's on the right. Check the same execution ID on both phones.

Moto G6 · HNS sellerPixel 9 · Bitcoin buyer
  1. 01Moto

    Open Shakescape

    Open the app and choose Wallet from the menu.

  2. 02Pixel

    Open Shakescape

    Open the app and choose Wallet from the menu.

  3. 03Moto

    Unlock and sync

    Unlock Wallet. In Bitcoin, tap SYNC and wait for a verified balance. Check that HNS is synced too.

  4. 04Pixel

    Unlock and sync

    Unlock Wallet. In Bitcoin, tap SYNC and wait for a verified balance. Check that HNS is synced too.

  5. 05Moto

    Set the offer terms

    Connect to an offer peer and enter 0.500546 HNS in exchange for 4,330 sats.

  6. 06Moto

    Publish the offer

    Review the amounts and publish. This shares the terms; it does not lock HNS yet.

  7. 07Pixel

    Find Moto's offer

    Connect to the same offer peer. Browse offers and match the offer ID and both amounts.

  8. 08Pixel

    Accept the offer

    Check the Bitcoin funding fee limit, then accept the exact offer.

  9. 09Moto

    Choose the response

    On Moto, select Pixel's response to the offer and countersign it. Wait for both HNS wallets to finish verifying the swap before funding.

  10. 10Pixel

    Review the Bitcoin lock

    Open execution af7211abb240… . Check the 4,330-sat lock and fee before approving the red Broadcast funding action.

  11. 11Pixel

    Broadcast the Bitcoin lock

    The Bitcoin funding transaction is submitted. Keep Bitcoin synced until it confirms.

  12. 12Moto

    Wait for Bitcoin confirmation

    Open the same execution ID. Moto shows 1 of 5 while it waits to verify Pixel's Bitcoin lock.

  13. 13Pixel

    Verify the Bitcoin lock

    Pixel verifies its funding transaction and the execution reaches 2 of 5.

  14. 14Moto

    Verify the Bitcoin lock

    Moto verifies the same transaction and can now prepare HNS funding.

  15. 15Moto

    Review the HNS lock

    Check the 0.500546-HNS lock and fee, then approve the red broadcast action.

  16. 16Moto

    Broadcast the HNS lock

    Moto submits the HNS funding transaction. Keep HNS synced while it waits for confirmation.

  17. 17Pixel

    Wait for HNS confirmations

    Pixel waits for two Handshake confirmations before the HNS can be claimed.

  18. 18Moto

    Verify HNS funding

    Moto verifies both confirmations. Its execution reaches 3 of 5.

  19. 19Pixel

    Prepare to receive HNS

    Pixel verifies the same blocks. Its execution reaches 3 of 5 and HNS redemption becomes available.

  20. 20Pixel

    Confirm the HNS claim

    Pixel's wallet claims HNS automatically. Recent activity shows the confirmed 0.486846-HNS credit.

  21. 21Moto

    Observe the swap secret

    Moto sees Pixel's HNS claim, verifies the revealed secret, and reaches 4 of 5. Its Bitcoin claim is ready.

  22. 22Pixel

    Wait for Bitcoin redemption

    Pixel shows 4 of 5 while it waits for Moto to claim the Bitcoin lock.

  23. 23Moto

    Follow the Bitcoin claim

    Moto's wallet broadcasts its Bitcoin claim. Find the pending transaction in Bitcoin activity and wait for confirmation.

  24. 24Pixel

    Verify the Bitcoin claim

    Pixel syncs Bitcoin and verifies Moto's claim transaction.

  25. 25Moto

    Confirm the Bitcoin credit

    Moto syncs Bitcoin and sees the confirmed 4,052-sat credit from the claim.

  26. 26Moto

    Confirm completion

    Execution af7211abb240… shows 5 of 5 steps completed and State: completed.

  27. 27Pixel

    Confirm completion

    The same execution shows 5 of 5 steps completed and State: completed.
