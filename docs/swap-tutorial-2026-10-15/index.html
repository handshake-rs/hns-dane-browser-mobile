Moto sells HNS · Pixel pays Bitcoin

Swap HNS for Bitcoin on two phones

Follow Moto on the left and Pixel on the right. Check the offer, amounts, and execution ID on both phones as you go.

Moto G6 · HNS sellerPixel 9 · Bitcoin buyer
  1. 01

    Open Shakescape

    Start Shakescape on both phones. Open the menu and choose Wallet.

  2. 02

    Unlock and sync both wallets

    Unlock Wallet on both phones. In Bitcoin, tap SYNC and wait for verified balances. Check that HNS is also synced before choosing swap terms.

  3. 03

    Find the offer through the relay

    Connect both phones to the same offer peer. Moto creates an offer for 0.500546 HNS in exchange for 4,330 sats. Pixel uses Browse offers and checks the offer ID and amounts.

  4. 04

    Review and accept the offer

    Moto reviews and publishes the offer. Pixel checks the amounts and maximum Bitcoin funding fee, then accepts that offer. Funding still requires a separate approval.

  5. 05

    Review Pixel's Bitcoin lock

    After Pixel accepts, Moto countersigns the chosen response. Wait for both HNS scans to finish, then open the matching execution on Pixel. Check the 4,330-sat lock, fee, and red final approval before broadcasting.

  6. 06

    Broadcast the Bitcoin lock

    Pixel submits the Bitcoin funding transaction. Keep both Bitcoin wallets synced until they verify it in a block.

  7. 07

    Verify the Bitcoin lock

    Both phones verify the same Bitcoin transaction. The execution reaches 2 of 5 steps, and Moto can prepare HNS funding.

  8. 08

    Review Moto's HNS lock

    On Moto, open the matching execution and choose Prepare HNS funding. Check the 0.500546-HNS lock, fee, and red final approval.

  9. 09

    Broadcast the HNS lock

    Moto submits the HNS funding transaction. Keep both HNS wallets synced while the lock waits for confirmation.

  10. 10

    Verify the second HNS block

    Wait for both phones to verify two HNS confirmations. The execution then reaches 3 of 5 and Pixel can redeem HNS.

  11. 11

    Pixel receives HNS

    Pixel's wallet submits the HNS claim automatically. Confirm the credit in recent activity and check that the execution reaches 4 of 5 on both phones.

  12. 12

    Follow Moto's Bitcoin claim

    Moto's wallet broadcasts the Bitcoin claim. Find it in Bitcoin activity and wait for confirmation; do not submit a second claim while it is pending.

  13. 13

    Sync the Bitcoin claim

    Run Bitcoin sync on both phones. Moto's confirmed activity shows a 4,052-sat credit from the claim.

  14. 14

    Confirm completion

    Open execution af7211abb240… on both phones. Both should show 5 of 5 steps completed and State: completed.
